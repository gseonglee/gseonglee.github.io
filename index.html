Gyuseong Lee

Computer Vision Researcher

I am a Computer Vision Research Scientist at LG Electronics (2024-present).

My research focuses on diffusion- and flow-based generative models, as well as data-centric approaches for vision models.

I received my M.S. in Computer Science from CVLAB, Korea University (2022–2024).
