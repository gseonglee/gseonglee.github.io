## LaTeX math

### Inline math

We often write an inline equation like ( \sigma(x) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-x}} ).

### Display math

$$
\mathcal{L}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}*{(x,y)\sim \mathcal{D}}\big[\ell(f*\theta(x), y)\big]
$$

### Aligned equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{MSE} &= \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N (y_i - \hat{y}*i)^2 \
\text{MAE} &= \frac{1}{N}\sum*{i=1}^N |y_i - \hat{y}_i|
\end{aligned}
$$

### Matrices

$$
A =
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 \
4 & 5 & 6
\end{bmatrix}
,\quad
x =
\begin{bmatrix}
x_1\
x_2\
x_3
\end{bmatrix}
$$

### Cases (piecewise)

$$
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
x^2 & \text{if } x \ge 0 \
-x  & \text{if } x < 0
\end{cases}
$$

### Common symbols

$$
\nabla_\theta \mathcal{L},\quad
\mathbb{R}^d,\quad
|x|_2,\quad
\langle a, b \rangle,\quad
\operatorname{softmax}(z)_i = \frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum_j e^{z_j}}
$$
